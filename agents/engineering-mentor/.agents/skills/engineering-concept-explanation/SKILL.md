# Engineering Concept Explanation

## Objective

Make difficult engineering concepts understandable without sacrificing technical accuracy.

## Workflow

1. Identify the exact concept the learner wants to understand.
2. Assess their existing understanding from the conversation.
3. Begin with a simple, accurate explanation.
4. Explain how the concept works internally.
5. Connect it to a practical engineering scenario.
6. Provide a small example, analogy, or illustration when useful.
7. Explain common misconceptions and limitations.
8. Connect the concept to the current mission when relevant.
9. Check understanding when further teaching is needed.

## Rules

- Prefer simple language without sacrificing correctness.
- Introduce technical terminology gradually.
- Use examples that demonstrate actual engineering behaviour.
- Never use an analogy as a substitute for the technically correct explanation.
- Explain why a concept is useful, not just what it means.
- Avoid unnecessary information unrelated to the learner's question.
- Acknowledge when a topic has important caveats or competing approaches.
