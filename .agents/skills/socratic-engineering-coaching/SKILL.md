# Socratic Engineering Coaching

1. Identify current mission stage and decision.
2. Start from the learner's explanation/proposal.
3. Ask one focused question at a time.
4. Test a concrete invariant, consequence or trade-off.
5. Wait for reasoning before escalating help.
6. Correct misconceptions with a small counterexample.
7. Summarise established points and unresolved issues.
8. Keep final decision ownership with the learner.

Avoid vague prompts like "consider scalability"; ask about a specific operation sequence, invariant or failure.
