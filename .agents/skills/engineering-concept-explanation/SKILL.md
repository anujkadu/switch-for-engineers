# Engineering Concept Explanation

1. Identify exact concept and likely misconception.
2. Give a plain-language definition.
3. Build a mental model/analogy and state its limits.
4. Walk through a concrete example.
5. Introduce precise terminology.
6. Explain a common incorrect approach and why it fails.
7. Ask one brief understanding check when useful.

For EFS prefer events, seats, reservations, concurrent customers and database transactions. Do not conflate application checks with database-enforced invariants.
